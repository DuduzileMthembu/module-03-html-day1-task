How I Can Make Difference In My Community

One of the biggest issues in my community is unemployment, this causes many young people without jobs and that leads to frustration. Many young people finish schools with qualifications but still can not find jobs because there are few opportunities available.

I would love to show youth that there are still opportunities around to build skills required by recruiters using technology. I believe partnering with local organisations like NYDA, Skill Hubs, Department of labour Ask businesses to take interns, even one intern at a time. Sharing information like posting on community groups and creating awareness of these opportunities.

This would benefit my community in many ways because young people will be able to receive information about job opportunities and resources, create groups where sharing information can take place. The more opportunities arise, the better it will be for everyone in the community.
